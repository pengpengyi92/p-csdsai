# From P-Trust to P-CSDSAI: open programming and systems research

**Statement timestamp:** `2026-11-14T04:14:18+08:00` (Asia/Shanghai, UTC+08:00).

P-Trust began as a trustworthy-AI research project and a read-only agent and repository inspection tool. The same public GitHub repository is now **P-CSDSAI**, covering **Computer Science, Data Science and Artificial Intelligence**. We are expanding the conversation, not replacing the detector or rewriting its history.

**Programming comes first.** We welcome Python, SQL, C++, Rust, kdb+/q, and other languages and tools. Bring coding questions, small runnable examples, data and query systems, algorithms, reproducible benchmarks, software engineering trade-offs, and implementation reports. We also welcome infrastructure, HPC, distributed compute, inference serving and scheduling, agent performance, post-training, and AI trust and safety. P-Trust remains the safety and inspection project inside this broader scope.

The **Department of Computer Science, Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (CSDSAI)** is part of **P University**, a private proprietary research initiative. The department has published papers and articles internally; those are not public publications. **PINF** and **P-Compute** are also private proprietary repositories. PINF studies AI infrastructure and operations; P-Compute studies high-performance computing, model serving and scheduling. P-CSDSAI may publish selected, explicitly cleared contributions from these sources. No private repository is required to participate or reproduce public work.

Join [Discussions](https://github.com/pengpengyi92/P-CSDSAI/discussions) for technical exchange, open an [Issue](https://github.com/pengpengyi92/P-CSDSAI/issues) for concrete work, and read [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) before a focused PR. Human and AI-assisted contributions are welcome. Use public or synthetic evidence, and distinguish a proposal from a measured result.

No internal paper, article, code, data or deployment settings changed in this update. The [previous P-Trust statement](2026-11-14-trustworthy-ai-community.md) remains a historical record.
